before you start.

Less guesswork. More useful records.

Do I still need to type anything?

You can enter a fill-up manually. AI photo auto-fill can reduce typing, but you may need to correct or complete details. Review every entry before saving.

Does Odomo have a paid plan?

Odomo currently offers a free plan. Premium will be added in the future.

What if a photo doesn’t read correctly?

Check the extracted details, correct any mistakes, and add anything missing before saving. A receipt and an odometer reading can provide different parts of a fuel entry.

Do reminders replace checking my vehicle?

No. Keep logging consistently, check your fuel level, and follow your vehicle’s service guidance. Reminders are a helpful prompt, not a guarantee of when fuel or service is needed.

How are my photos and records handled?

Odomo uses accounts and cloud sync to keep your records connected. Photo auto-fill sends your selected images to an external AI service for processing. See our Privacy Policy or contact support with questions.

Where can I find download information?

App Store and Google Play listings are pending. Download links and device compatibility will be available once the listings are ready.
