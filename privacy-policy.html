1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Odomo handles information when you use the app, account services, cloud sync, AI photo processing, app analytics, crash reports, and this website. Odomo is operated by Rahmat Nugraha under the Marikitaparty brand. Contact support-odomo@marikita.party about this policy or your information.

Read this policy alongside our Terms & Conditions and Close Account instructions.

2. Information you provide

Odomo processes information needed to provide the features you use:

  • Account information: your registration email, account identifier, and information you supply to manage or recover your account.
  • Vehicle and activity records: vehicle details, odometer readings, fuel amounts and costs, dates, service records, notes, and reminders you enter or confirm.
  • Selected photos: fuel receipts and dashboard or odometer images you choose for AI-assisted entry, along with information extracted from them.
  • Preferences: settings such as units and currency.
  • Support messages: information you send when asking for help or exercising your privacy rights.

Provide only information you are entitled to use. Receipts and photos may contain personal details unrelated to a fuel entry; crop or redact those details before submitting an image when possible.

3. Information collected automatically

Odomo sends a small amount of technical information about how the app is used, when it fails, and which store link led to an installation. We use it to understand which features are used, to find and fix crashes, and to measure whether our store links work. It is not used to show you advertising, and we do not sell it, except for the optional ad measurement described under “Ad measurement (optional)” below, which is off unless you turn it on.

Analytics events and crash reports are received by servers we operate ourselves, not by a third-party analytics or crash-reporting service, and they are sent over an encrypted connection. The one exception is the optional ad measurement described below, which is off unless you turn it on. This information never includes your records, your photos, the text read from a photo, your name, your email address, or your account identifier.

App analytics

The app records a fixed list of usage events: that it was opened, which onboarding step was shown, that a scan started and how it resolved, that a paywall or purchase step was reached, that an entry was added, and similar product events. Each event carries the platform (Android, iOS or web), often the app language, and a short value from a fixed list — an onboarding step, a scan tier, a fallback reason, a paywall placement, a product id, an entry type, or a bucketed count such as “6–20 entries” or “51+”. Values come from fixed lists or are grouped into ranges; the app never sends free text, values you type, vehicle details, odometer readings, prices, fuel volumes, station or place names, notes, or text read from a photo.

To keep one installation’s events together, the app generates a random identifier for this installation on first launch. It is not your account identifier, not a hardware or advertising identifier, and not derived from your email or any other personal detail. We do not link it to your account or combine it with data from other apps or websites. The only campaign information we keep is described under install attribution below. If you turn on ad measurement, the campaign name, ad network and ad set that brought you to Odomo may also appear as anonymous properties on these events; they are never identifiers.

You can turn app analytics off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Anonymous analytics. App analytics is on by default; turning it off stops all analytics requests from this device and clears events that were waiting to be sent. The choice is stored on this device and is not part of your account, so signing out or using Odomo elsewhere does not switch it back on. Turning it off does not delete events that were already received; see sections 10 and 11.

Crash reports

If the app fails, it sends a crash report. A report contains the error type and message, the code stack, the screen or route involved, the app version and build, the operating-system version and device model, and basic session counters. Our crash server also records the connecting IP address.

Before a report leaves your device, a filter removes photo and receipt references and file paths, access tokens, email addresses, account, device and session identifiers, request headers, bodies and query strings, any user field, your device’s name, and the values of local variables in the stack. Photographs, screenshots, the on-screen view hierarchy and session recordings are never attached, and the app does not send performance traces or failed-request contents.

Crash reports are not linked to your account or to the analytics installation identifier. They are sent automatically and there is currently no separate setting for them. See section 10 for how long we keep them.

Install attribution

To understand which campaign or link led to an installation, Odomo asks the store once, at first launch, for the install referrer (Android) or an attribution token (iOS). From the answer it keeps only campaign values — utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content and utm_term — and which store provided them. The raw referrer and the Apple token are discarded immediately and never stored or sent. Advertising and click identifiers that a referrer may carry, such as Google click ids, are not kept.

Attribution runs only while app analytics is on, and it is sent once. With analytics off, the app does not read the referrer or ask Apple for an attribution token at all.

Ad measurement (optional)

If you turn on Settings → Privacy → Attribution and ad measurement, Odomo shares limited data with AppsFlyer, our measurement partner, so we can learn which advertisement led to your install and which ads lead to subscriptions. This switch is off by default in every region and is separate from Anonymous analytics: turning one on or off does not change the other.

The data includes your IP address, device identifiers, your device’s advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, or the iOS advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks), and events such as install, session and purchase. Purchase events (subscription start, purchase and refund) are sent server to server by our billing provider, Adapty, together with the AppsFlyer identifier. We do not collect the Android ID. AppsFlyer processes this data on our behalf and may pass conversion information to the advertising networks we use (currently Meta) so that they can measure and optimise our ads. We do not send your account details, vehicle data, entries, photos or scans to AppsFlyer.

We use this only to measure and improve advertising. Nothing in Odomo depends on it. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. Turning it off stops the AppsFlyer SDK and prevents it from starting on later launches, but it does not recall data that was already sent. To ask for deletion of data sent to AppsFlyer, contact us at the address in section 11 and we will pass your request on to AppsFlyer. AppsFlyer keeps data under its own retention terms.

Analytics on this website

The marketing website uses the same self-hosted service, without cookies. It records a page view for each page you open (the page path and page title, and the page you came from), the store badge you click if any, and campaign values in the link that brought you here. The tracker keeps its identifier in memory for the page only and stores nothing in your browser, so this website cannot recognise you across visits.

4. How your information is used

Account information supports sign-in, account access, and cloud synchronization. Vehicle records support your history, running-cost calculations, and reminders. Selected images are processed to suggest entry details, which you review before saving. Support information is used to respond to your request and verify account ownership when necessary.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing is based on providing the service you request, your permission for optional processing, or applicable legal obligations. Withdrawing permission for photo processing does not prevent you from entering records manually.

5. Accounts and cloud synchronization

Odomo provides accounts and cloud synchronization. Records synchronized with your account are handled by online infrastructure and are not confined to your phone. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not, by itself, close your account or delete synchronized data. Section 14 describes the optional alternative where the same synchronized copy is kept in your own Google Drive instead.

To close your account and request deletion of associated records, follow the Close Account instructions. Contact support for information about the infrastructure providers and locations relevant to your account.

6. Vision AI and external processing

Odomo uses vision-capable large language models (LLM Vision AI) to read the fuel receipts and odometer photos you select. Processing can involve models such as OpenAI GPT Luna and Google Gemini Flash, accessed through an AI aggregator provider that routes requests to the selected model provider.

Your selected image, the instructions needed to extract entry details, and the resulting response pass through the aggregator and model provider. This processing happens outside your device. Providers or model versions may change as availability and suitable processing routes change.

Use photo processing only for images you intend to submit. You can enter the details manually instead. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect; review, correct, and complete the entry before saving it.

7. Our Zero Data Retention commitment

We are committed to enabling Zero Data Retention (ZDR) for Odomo’s AI processing through eligible aggregator and model-provider configurations. This commitment concerns the images, prompts, and responses sent for model processing. Our intended configuration excludes retaining this request content for model training or continued storage after processing.

ZDR depends on the selected provider, endpoint, features, and processing route. Provider requirements and legally required safety handling can affect its scope. The commitment is not a claim that every provider or model configuration automatically supports ZDR. Contact support for the currently applicable processing arrangements.

ZDR for external AI requests is separate from your Odomo account data. Entries you confirm and save, and any attachments you choose to retain with those entries, may remain in Odomo’s storage or cloud sync until you delete them or close your account. ZDR does not mean that your saved fuel history disappears after each scan.

8. Service providers and sharing

The analytics and crash-reporting services described in section 3 are not third-party products. We run them ourselves on a server we control, and we do not share what they receive with analytics vendors, advertising networks, data brokers, or any other third party. The only exception is the optional ad measurement described in section 3, which is off unless you turn it on. Traffic to that server passes through Cloudflare’s network, which acts as our network provider. We do not sell personal information.

Online infrastructure providers handle account and synchronized-record services. The AI aggregator and model providers handle selected photo-processing requests. Access to information should be limited to what each service needs for its role. If a paid plan is introduced, the relevant purchase platform may also process transaction information under its own terms.

If you turn on ad measurement (section 3), AppsFlyer acts as our service provider and processes the data described there on our behalf, and may pass conversion information to the advertising networks we use (currently Meta). This happens only if you opt in.

Information may also need to be disclosed to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request, or to address account-security issues.

9. Processing locations and security

Cloud and AI providers, and AppsFlyer if you turn on ad measurement, may process information outside the country where you live. Contact support for provider and location details or questions about applicable safeguards.

Analytics events and crash reports are processed on a server we operate ourselves, not by a third-party analytics or crash-reporting platform, except for the optional ad measurement described in section 3, which is off unless you turn it on. The resulting dashboards are not reachable from the public internet; only the ingest endpoints, events.marikita.party and errors.marikita.party (with their staging equivalents), accept data. Contact support for the current hosting location of that server.

The app ships the client credentials it needs to write analytics events to that endpoint. They are not a secret in the security sense, because anyone who inspects the app can read them. They are the write credential for our project: they are not intended to read stored data or open a dashboard, and they give no access to your account. We rely on the endpoint rather than on secrecy — only the ingest path is exposed, requests are restricted by origin and rate limited — so a copied credential could at most add misleading events to aggregate counts.

Keep your account credentials secure and report suspected unauthorized access promptly. We are committed to using appropriate safeguards for account and service information. No online service can promise absolute protection against every security risk.

10. Retention and deletion

We keep account and synchronized records to provide the service while you maintain your account, subject to applicable deletion requests. Closing your account requests removal of your profile and associated vehicle records, saved entries, reminders, and stored attachments from active service storage.

We have not yet fixed a retention period for analytics events or crash reports. Until we do, they remain stored on our self-hosted systems (data you choose to share with AppsFlyer through ad measurement is kept under AppsFlyer’s own retention terms) and this policy states no period; when a period is set it will be published here. Turning app analytics off stops new events but does not remove events that were already received. Likewise, turning off ad measurement does not recall data already sent to AppsFlyer.

Some information may need to remain for a legal obligation or a necessary security purpose. Backup deletion and the applicable completion period depend on the storage arrangements. We will explain the expected processing time and any relevant retained data when handling your request.

See Close Account for the request route and the difference between deleting specific records and closing your account.

11. Your choices and privacy rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, or information about the processing of your personal data by contacting support-odomo@marikita.party. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent.

We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before releasing or deleting information. We will handle requests within the period required by applicable law. Your statutory privacy rights do not depend on purchasing Premium.

You can stop app analytics and install attribution at any time in Settings → Privacy → Anonymous analytics, as described in section 3. Crash reports currently have no separate setting. Ad measurement is a separate, optional switch, Settings → Privacy → Attribution and ad measurement, which is off by default and can be turned off at any time.

To ask us to delete analytics events or crash reports, contact us using the address above. Because those streams are not linked to your account, we may need information from you to find the records, and we may not be able to identify all of them; we will explain what we can do in your case. For data sent to AppsFlyer through ad measurement, we will pass your deletion request on to AppsFlyer.

You can manage camera and photo permissions through your device settings and use manual entry without submitting an image for AI processing.

14. Google Drive sync backup

For cloud synchronization, Odomo can also keep the copy of your synchronized records in your own Google Drive instead of Odomo’s infrastructure. In that case the app writes an encrypted copy into the hidden application data folder that Google provides to Odomo inside your Drive (the app-private folder named appDataFolder). This route is optional: it is used only if the app offers it, you choose it, and you grant the Google access described below. Otherwise synchronization works as described in section 5.

The route uses a single Google scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. That scope reaches only Odomo’s own application data folder. It does not allow Odomo to list, open, change, or delete any other file in your Drive, including files you created yourself and anything in shared drives.

The copy is stored as a few files with opaque names. Its contents are encrypted with a key held by Odomo’s account service, and that key is never sent to Google. Google cannot read your records, entry values, vehicle details, or photos; only Odomo can decrypt the copy. Google’s own systems can see that the app created files in your folder, their names, their sizes, and their modification times, plus the numbers and commit times inside the small index file that orders the copy. Those values show roughly how much and how often you synchronize and nothing about what you store.

The folder counts toward the storage quota of the Google Account you connect. If that quota is full, the Drive copy stops updating and the app reports a synchronization error; the records on your device are not affected. Odomo’s servers never receive your Drive traffic or your Google access token, and the token stays on your device.

You can stop this route and remove the copy at any time:

  • Revoke Odomo’s access to your Google Account under Google Account → Data & privacy → Third-party apps & services. Revocation stops all Drive use immediately, and the app asks you to grant access again if you later want the route back.
  • Delete Odomo’s hidden application data under Google Drive settings → Manage apps, which removes the files Odomo stored in your Drive.

Revoking access does not by itself delete the encrypted files; until they are removed they stay unreadable to Google. Odomo also deletes the Drive copy when your account returns to Odomo’s own cloud synchronization. Closing your Odomo account does not delete files held in your Google Drive, because they belong to your Google Account; use the steps above for those.

Odomo’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The Drive copy is used only to synchronize the records you keep in Odomo: it is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred to anyone else.

15. Scan feedback

After a scan, you can optionally tap thumbs up or thumbs down to rate the result. If you do, we receive: your rating; the scan type (receipt, pump or odometer); how the photo was taken (live, gallery or still); how the scan locator and the extraction performed (outcome, method and duration); which fields you corrected (a yes or no for each field, not the values); the app version and platform; and an optional short comment of up to 200 characters, only if you type one. Please do not put personal data in a comment.

We do not send or store your photo, the recognized text, the extracted values, or your email or account identifiers with this feedback. The sender is stored only as a salted hash that is not shown to reviewers.

We use this feedback to improve scan accuracy. It is kept for 180 days and then deleted automatically. Feedback is optional and never affects scanning, saving or your plan limits.
