1. Delete your account in Odomo

To close your account from the app:

  1. Open the You page.
  2. Tap Account (your name or email).
  3. Choose Delete Account and follow the prompts.

Request deletion through support

You can request closure of your Odomo account and deletion of its associated information by emailing support-odomo@marikita.party from the email address registered to your account. Use the subject Odomo account deletion request.

  1. Open the email draft below, or write to support directly.
  2. Include your registered email and state that you want to close your account and delete its associated data.
  3. Send the message from your email app. Support will verify ownership if needed and confirm the next steps.
Prepare deletion request ↗

Opens a draft in your email app. This website does not delete your account automatically.

If you cannot access your registered email, explain that to support. We may need additional verification before acting. Never send your password or a sign-in verification code.

2. Before you close your account

Save any information you need to keep before requesting deletion. Closure ends access to the account and associated cloud history once it is completed. Signing out, deleting the app, or revoking camera permission is not the same as closing your account.

If you need help recovering access or correcting information instead, contact support before requesting closure.

3. What your request covers

Your closure request covers the account profile and associated records in Odomo’s active service storage, including:

  • Registration email and account information.
  • Saved vehicles, fuel and service records, odometer readings, notes, and reminders.
  • Account-linked settings and stored attachments or photos.

External AI request processing is addressed separately by our Zero Data Retention commitment. Closing your account does not mean an email request can undo processing that has already taken place.

4. Verification, timing, and retained information

Support will confirm the information needed to verify the account, the expected completion period, and when the request has been processed. We handle requests within the time required by applicable law. Contact support for the status of a pending request.

Information that must be retained for a legal obligation or a necessary security purpose may be kept separately for that purpose. Backup removal follows the applicable storage-retention arrangements. Ask support for the scope and duration of any retention relevant to your request.

5. Delete specific data instead

You can ask to remove particular vehicle records or account information without closing the whole account. Email support-odomo@marikita.party, identify the records you want removed, and make clear that you want to keep your account. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting.

6. Free plan and future subscriptions

Odomo currently offers a free plan. Premium is planned for the future, so there is no current Premium subscription to cancel.

If paid subscriptions become available later, closing an Odomo account may not cancel billing through an app store or another purchase platform. Cancel any such subscription through its purchase platform as well as requesting account closure.

7. Questions

For account closure, selective deletion, or access problems, contact support-odomo@marikita.party. See the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for related information.
